Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vistasilica collects, uses and protects the information you submit.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, request a sample or register to download documents, we collect the information you provide — name, company, email, phone, country and project requirements.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide selection, samples and technical support;
  • With your consent, to send documents and updates relevant to your track;
  • To improve our products and services.

Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties. We share it only where necessary to provide the service, and require those parties to observe corresponding confidentiality obligations.

Your rights (GDPR)

For EU users, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent or object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@vistasilica.com.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, and the site transmits over HTTPS.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact privacy@vistasilica.com.

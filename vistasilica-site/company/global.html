A tiered regional strategy

Tier one

Local formulation plants, blenders, OEM/ODM, independent refineries, licensed formulators. Need sample onboarding, application support, flexible packaging and stable repeat.

→ Push: enter testing fast, form a first order and continuous repeat.

Tier two

Technical distributors, licensed importers, regional landing partners. Need an importable product line, document packs and joint-development support.

→ Target: secure the landing end first, then bring in plant projects.

Tier three

Inbound inquiries, referrals from existing customers, opportunistic projects. Need clear screening, fast quotes and minimal-resource response.

→ Respond only, no upfront investment.

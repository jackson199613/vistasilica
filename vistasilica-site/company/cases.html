Feed additives · Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia · premix plant

Need
Unstable liquid-additive powdering, caking in storage
Solution
High-absorption carrier silica + anti-caking; grade fixed after main/comparison samples
Result
Stable liquid load, fewer caking complaints, moved into multi-grade repeat
Agrochemical · South Asia

South Asia · agrochemical formulator

Need
Slow WG disintegration, SC bottle residue
Solution
Sampled separately for WG disintegration and SC rheology, with lab trials
Result
Disintegration on target, bottle residue improved, expanded from one form to several
Oil refining · Middle East

Middle East · independent refinery

Need
High residual phosphorus in imported crude, high bleaching-earth use
Solution
Silica-gel dephosphorization + bleaching-earth combined approach, starting from current data
Result
Filtration pressure drop and earth use fell, steady repeat by line
Oral care · Latin America

Latin America · toothpaste OEM

Need
Import substitution, paste stability
Solution
Parallel lab trials of abrasive + thickener dual route

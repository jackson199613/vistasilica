Solution 06

Personal Care Mattifying Solution

For personal-care plants with formulators, labs and OEM/ODM capability. We provide comparison samples and onboarding around oil absorption, particle size, matte level and skin feel.

Help customers deliver an effect — not just sell powder.

Core value

  • Help customers balance oil control, matte, skin feel and stability for the target product positioning.
  • Reduce formulation trial-and-error and repeat sampling through comparison of oil absorption, particle size and powder behavior.
  • Make it easier for OEM/ODM plants to turn sample results into producible color, foundation and sun-care formulations.

Who it's for

  • OEM/ODM color-cosmetic plants, skincare contract manufacturers, sun-care and foundation plants.
  • Small-batch, multi-SKU plants with a formulation chemist and lab.
  • One-stop private-label platforms spanning color, oral and health categories.

Recommended grade directions

High oil absorptionSpherical · soft focusFine · matte

Typical benchmarks: Evonik SPHERILEX® / ACEMATT® personal-care and cosmetic silica (oil control, soft focus, skin feel), Tosoh Silica cosmetic silica series, Solvay high-performance personal-care specialty silica.

We don't replicate brand premiums. We enter through niche applications, second-source / backup supply and multiple grades, and differentiate with profile screening, problem diagnosis and sample conversion.

Validation guide

Test dimensionWhat to watchWhat it tells the customer
Oil absorptionWhether target oil-control performance is metJudges use experience
Particle size & matteWhether powder feel and appearance grade are affectedJudges makeup effect
Skin feelWhether it is fine, smooth and non-draggyJudges premium perception
StabilityWhether pressing, emulsification or long-term storage are affectedJudges manufacturability

Technical service

  • Application review — confirm process type, target load, current pain points and existing solution; define validation metrics.
  • Sample onboarding — provide main and comparison samples around the key metrics, supporting multi-grade screening.
  • Application follow-up — help trace the source of issues along the lab-to-pilot path.
  • Repeat & expand — start with one project, then extend to more systems and SKUs.

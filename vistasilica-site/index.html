Feed Additive Flow & Carrier

Premixes · Vitamin blends · Trace minerals · Liquid additive powdering

We don't sell silica as a filler. We focus on flow, anti-caking, liquid absorption and dispersion uniformity to make premix and additive systems easier to process and store.

Food Powder Flow

Seasonings · Powdered beverages · Soups · Dry blends

For food processors genuinely in powder compounding, dry blending and post-spray-drying — solving real caking, flow, dispersion and batch-stability problems rather than selling generic filler.

Make the powder flow — not just add one more ingredient.

Oral Care Silica

Toothpaste · Oral-care pastes · OEM / private-label filling

For plants that actually compound and fill their own toothpaste. Instead of quoting one abrasive price, we build a testable screening path across cleaning, structure, appearance and stability using both abrasive and thickener routes.

See abrasive and thickener as two lines together.

Agrochemical Carrier

WP/WG inert carrier · SC anti-caking / rheology

Only for local formulators that already hold registration and run their own lines. We improve absorption, disintegration, sedimentation and anti-caking in WP/WG/SC formulations.

Only inertness and rheology — never registration or efficacy.

Edible Oil Refining

Edible oil refining · Dephosphorization · Desoaping · Filtration

For independent refineries with real refining lines processing imported crude oil, focused on lowering residual phosphorus, filtration pressure drop and bleaching-earth consumption — a more cost-effective, stable refining combination.

Replace single-price comparison with a combined approach.

The problems we solve

Three real pain points in overseas ingredient sourcing

01

Can't find a truly fitting supplier

Many suppliers only quote prices — they don't understand the application or the customer's process boundaries.

→ Screen by track and country first, then sample by application to cut wasted development.
02

High first-order risk

Customers worry about trial-and-error, import process, batch stability and no repeat after sampling.

→ Lower trial cost with small samples, multiple grades, consolidation, full docs and staged onboarding.
03

Low overseas development efficiency

Many countries, mixed customers and uneven inquiry quality scatter business effort.

→ Advance by tier and concentrate resources on high-fit, high-repeat tracks.

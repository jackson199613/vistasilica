Solution 01

Feed Additive Flow & Carrier Solution

We don't sell silica as a filler. We focus on flow, anti-caking, liquid absorption and dispersion uniformity to make premix and additive systems easier to process and store.

Turn liquids into powder. Keep premixes stable.

Core value

  • Convert liquid vitamins, organic acids and antioxidants into free-flowing powders or premixes more reliably.
  • Reduce caking, bridging and unstable flow of premixes and trace-mineral blends during production, packing and storage.
  • Improve dispersion uniformity of additives in powder systems, cutting mixing inconsistency, rework and batch variation.

On VS-C200, an oil absorption of 240–290 g/100g (ISO 4652) and BET surface area of 170–230 m²/g (ISO 9277) support high liquid loading and carrier capacity for premix and liquid-additive powdering.

Who it's for

  • Vitamin premix, trace-mineral premix and animal-nutrition additive plants.
  • Blenders powdering choline chloride, acidifiers, antioxidants and anti-mold liquid or semi-liquid additives.
  • Customers with a lab and application engineers who can discuss absorption, flowability and uniformity.

Recommended grade directions

Carrier · high absorptionFlow aid · fineAnti-caking

Typical benchmarks: Evonik SIPERNAT® series (e.g. SIPERNAT® 22, common in premix carrier / flow-aid / anti-caking), QEMETICA/PQ Flo-Gard® feed grade silica, W. R. Grace SYLOID® food/feed carrier and anti-caking grades.

We don't replicate brand premiums. We enter through niche applications, second-source / backup supply and multiple grades, and differentiate with profile screening, problem diagnosis and sample conversion.

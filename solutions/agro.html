Solution 04

Agrochemical Carrier Solution

Only for local formulators that already hold registration and run their own lines. We improve absorption, disintegration, sedimentation and anti-caking in WP/WG/SC formulations.

Only inertness and rheology — never registration or efficacy.

Core value

  • Help formulators improve absorption, disintegration, sedimentation and anti-caking in WP/WG/SC, raising formulation stability.
  • Reduce dust, hard sediment, bottle residue and poor dispersion, improving production and end-use experience.
  • Speed the move from lab evaluation to formulation onboarding by testing around the specific dosage form.

Who it's for

  • Local formulators holding CIB&RC, DPP or equivalent registration / manufacturing licenses.
  • Agrochemical plants with WP, WG or SC formulation lines and a lab.
  • Technical customers who can clearly discuss absorption, disintegration, sedimentation and dust control.

Recommended grade directions

WP inert carrierWG disintegratingSC rheology / anti-caking

Typical benchmarks: Evonik precipitated silica for agrochemical carriers, Quechen Silicon precipitated-silica carrier grades, Madhu Silica precipitated silica carrier grades.

We don't replicate brand premiums. We enter through niche applications, second-source / backup supply and multiple grades, and differentiate with profile screening, problem diagnosis and sample conversion.

Validation guide

Test dimensionWhat to watchWhat it tells the customer
Absorption / bulk densityWhether the target active load is supportedJudges formulation feasibility
WG disintegrationWhether target disintegration time and dispersion are metJudges end-use experience
SC stabilityWhether sedimentation, caking and bottle residue are reducedJudges shelf stability
Dust controlWhether production and packing improveJudges plant workability

Technical service

  • Application review — confirm process type, target load, current pain points and existing solution; define validation metrics.
  • Sample onboarding — provide main and comparison samples around the key metrics, supporting multi-grade screening.
  • Application follow-up — help trace the source of issues along the lab-to-pilot path.
  • Repeat & expand — start with one project, then extend to more systems and SKUs.

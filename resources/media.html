Inside the plant

From reactor to bag

Six steps in our Longyan facility. Each one decides a parameter you will later read on the COA.

  1. Reaction

    Sodium silicate and acid are metered into the reactor under controlled pH and temperature — the step that sets particle size and pore structure before anything else can be adjusted.

  2. Filtration & washing

    The precipitate is filtered and washed until conductivity confirms the salts are out. Residual electrolyte is what causes downstream pH drift in a customer’s system.

  3. Spray drying

    Slurry is atomised into the drying tower. Inlet temperature and feed rate here determine bulk density and how the powder will later flow in a silo.

  4. Milling

    Dried agglomerates are milled to the target D50. This is where an abrasive grade and a flow-aid grade separate from the same base material.

  5. QC laboratory

    Every lot is tested for BET surface area, oil absorption, particle size, pH and loss on drying before release — the values that appear on your COA.

  6. Packaging

    25 kg bags, batch-coded and palletised. Small-batch and multi-grade consolidation are handled at this stage, not as an afterthought.

Customer visits & audits

What an audit day looks like

The route customers walk when they audit our Longyan facility — start to finish.

  1. Arrival

    Customers are met at the plant entrance in Longyan. Most audits start the morning after landing in Xiamen — roughly two hours away by road.

  2. Safety briefing

    PPE, evacuation routes and photography rules before anyone enters the production area. Auditors usually want to see that this step exists at all.

  3. Reaction workshop

    The reactor hall — where pH and temperature control are demonstrated live rather than described in a slide deck.

  4. QC laboratory

    Instruments, methods and retained samples. Customers can ask for a specific batch to be pulled and re-tested in front of them.

  5. Sample room

    Grades side by side, so a formulator can see and handle the difference between an abrasive, a carrier and a flow aid in one place.

  6. Technical meeting

    Process parameters, target metrics and packaging are worked through with the technical team — usually where the trial plan gets agreed.

  7. Before departure

    The part nobody schedules. Several of our longest-running accounts started with a visit like this one.

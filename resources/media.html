Inside the plant

From reactor to bag

Eight steps in our Longyan facility. Each one decides a parameter you will later read on the COA — and the last two are what customers walk through when they audit us.

  1. Reaction

    Sodium silicate and acid are metered into the reactor under controlled pH and temperature — the step that sets particle size and pore structure before anything else can be adjusted.

  2. Filtration & washing

    The precipitate is filtered and washed until conductivity confirms the salts are out. Residual electrolyte is what causes downstream pH drift in a customer’s system.

  3. Spray drying

    Slurry is atomised into the drying tower. Inlet temperature and feed rate here determine bulk density and how the powder will later flow in a silo.

  4. Milling

    Dried agglomerates are milled to the target D50. This is where an abrasive grade and a flow-aid grade separate from the same base material.

  5. QC laboratory

    Every lot is tested for BET surface area, oil absorption, particle size, pH and loss on drying before release — the values that appear on your COA.

  6. Packaging

    25 kg bags, batch-coded and palletised. Small-batch and multi-grade consolidation are handled at this stage, not as an afterthought.

  7. Safety first

    PPE, evacuation routes and area rules are covered before anyone enters production — the step auditors check exists before they check anything else.

  8. Open to audit

    Customers walk the reactor hall themselves. pH and temperature control are demonstrated live rather than described in a slide deck.
